Short summaries of terms from C5:2026. The catalogue itself is authoritative. Each entry gives the section.

## Basic criteria

Each criterion consists of one or more basic sub-criteria (`01B`, `02B`, …). Together they form the basic criterion and are read in combination, not in isolation. (Section 2.1)

## Additional criteria: sharpening and complementing

Additional sub-criteria come in two kinds (section 2.1):

- **Sharpening** (`01AS`, …): addresses the same aspect as a specific basic sub-criterion, with stricter requirements, and replaces it.
- **Complementing** (`01AC`, …): introduces a new aspect that none of the basic sub-criteria address.

## Complementary customer criteria

For selected criteria, the catalogue describes where cloud service customers have to cooperate within their own area of responsibility. The catalogue's example: with infrastructure services, the customer typically installs security updates for the operating system; with software services, the provider typically does. The list is explicitly not exhaustive. (Section 2.1)

## General Conditions (GC)

Information on the general conditions of the cloud service, such as applicable law, locations, availability and how government requests are handled. It helps customers assess whether the service suits their use case and makes reports comparable. (Section 4)

## Type 1 and Type 2 reports

- **Type 1**: covers the design of the controls at a specified date.
- **Type 2**: additionally covers the operating effectiveness of the controls during a specified period.

Type 1 reports are limited to initial engagements. After that, a Type 2 report is required, covering at least three and normally at most twelve months. (Section 3.4.4)

## Subservice organisation, carve-out and inclusive method

A subservice organisation is a service organisation used by the cloud service provider whose services are relevant to the C5 criteria and whose own controls are needed, together with the provider's, to meet the criteria. (Section 1.2.2)

- **Inclusive method**: the subservice organisation's controls are included in the description and in the audit.
- **Carve-out method**: they are excluded. The description then identifies, among other things, which controls are expected at the subservice organisation and how the provider monitors them.
